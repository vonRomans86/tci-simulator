Model parameters for this patient
Volumes in L, clearances in L/min, ke0 in min⁻¹. Algorithm: 2-s step; in effect-site mode the rate is the highest that keeps the predicted Ce at or below target over the next 20 minutes (Shafer–Gregg-type approach), capped by the maximum pump rate.
Model sources
  • Marsh: Marsh B et al. Br J Anaesth 1991;67:41–8 (ke0 0.26/min, Diprifusor). Modified Marsh: ke0 1.2/min, Struys MMRF et al. Anesthesiology 2000;92:399–406.
  • Schnider: Schnider TW et al. Anesthesiology 1998;88:1170–82 and 1999;90:1502–16. Lean body mass by James.
  • Eleveld propofol: Eleveld DJ et al. Br J Anaesth 2018;120:942–59 (arterial samples, arterial ke0). FFM by Al-Sallami.
  • Paedfusor: Absalom A et al. Br J Anaesth 2003;91:507–13. Kataria: Kataria BK et al. Anesthesiology 1994;80:104–22. Paediatric ke0 (0.91 and 0.41/min): Muñoz HR et al. Anesthesiology 2004;101:1269–74.
  • Minto: Minto CF et al. Anesthesiology 1997;86:10–23. Eleveld remifentanil: Eleveld DJ et al. Anesthesiology 2017;126:1005–18.
  • Gepts (sufentanil): Gepts E et al. Anesthesiology 1995;83:1194–204. Maitre (alfentanil): Maitre PO et al. Anesthesiology 1987;66:3–12.
  • PTOL Bouillon: Bouillon TW et al. Anesthesiology 2004;100:1353–72; hierarchical model parameters as in Hannivoort LN et al. (Ce50 propofol 8.48 µg/mL, Ce50 remifentanil 1.16 ng/mL, γ 3.46), as used by van den Berg JP et al. J Clin Monit Comput 2021;35:815–25.
  • PTOL Kern: Kern SE et al. Anesthesiology 2004;100:1373–81, Greco model (C50 propofol 5.6 µg/mL, C50 remifentanil 48.9 ng/mL, slope 2.2, α 33.2), as reported by Johnson KB et al. Anesth Analg 2010.
  • BIS: Bouillon 2004 interaction model (C50 propofol 4.47 µg/mL, C50 remifentanil 19.3 ng/mL, γ 1.43, E0 97.4).
  • The response surfaces were estimated with propofol (Schnider) and remifentanil (Minto): with other PK models the Ce values are not directly equivalent.
